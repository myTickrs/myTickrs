# Translation glossary

The terms every translation uses the same way, so a word means one thing across the app. Change a
term here first, then everywhere it appears.

**Never translated:** ticker symbols, index symbols (^SPX), OCC option symbols, currency codes, ISO dates in examples,

brand names (myTickrs, Finnhub, Twelve Data, Google, Microsoft), regulatory fee names (SEC, TAF, ORF),

FIFO/ACB, and ITM/OTM/ATM in the European languages and Korean (Chinese uses 实值/虚值/平值 and
價內/價外/價平; Korean spells them out as 내가격/외가격/등가격 where there is room).

**Traditional Chinese** follows Taiwan usage and is translated from English on its own, not
converted from Simplified: the finance vocabulary differs (期权 → 選擇權, 行权 → 履約, 股息 → 股利,
展期 → 轉倉). Hong Kong and Macau readers get it too.

**Register:** French, German, Italian and Spanish address the reader formally where the language
expects it (vous, Sie) and informally where product UIs usually do (tu in Italian and Spanish).

**Korean** uses the formal 합니다 style in sentences and plain nouns on buttons and labels
(저장, 취소), with the terms Korean brokerages use for options (환매수, 전매도, 행사가).
